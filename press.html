02 / Profile

Danger Wølf (one part zine, one part experimental instrumental project) is the creation of artist, producer, and audio-visual collagist Dan Duarte.

Danger Wølf himself is the unexpected hero of this noir, offbeat crime comedy. Imagine Spy vs. Spy meets Inspector Gadget meets Tokyo Vice.

The story takes place in New Jet City. Each track pulls from records, movies, cartoons, and old video games, stitched together like clues from a case file.
